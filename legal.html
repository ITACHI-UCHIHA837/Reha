Frequently Asked Questions

Shipping & Delivery
How long will it take to receive my order?
Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days. Expedited shipping takes 1-2 business days. International shipping can take anywhere from 7-21 days depending on the destination.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping costs and delivery times will be calculated at checkout based on your location. Please note that international customers are responsible for any customs duties or taxes.
How can I track my order?
Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to track your package. You can also view tracking information by logging into your account on our website.
Returns & Exchanges
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have the original tags attached. Final sale items and intimates cannot be returned.
How do I start a return or exchange?
To initiate a return or exchange, please contact our support team with your order number and email address, and we will guide you through the process.
Sizing & Fit
How do I know what size to order?
We provide a detailed Size Guide on every product page with exact measurements. If you are between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for a looser fit or sizing down for a more tailored look.
Orders & Payment
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
We process orders very quickly. If you need to make a change or cancel your order, please contact our support team within 1 hour of placing it. Once an order has been processed for shipping, it cannot be changed.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and popular digital wallets. All transactions are securely encrypted.
